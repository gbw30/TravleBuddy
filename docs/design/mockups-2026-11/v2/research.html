Font pairing

A plain sans for reading, character in the headline

12of 12 set all reading text in a plain sans-serif
6of 12 add a monospace for small labels and numbers
4of 12 use a serif for headlines or one accent word

Body text is 15 to 18px and softened to 60 to 85% strength, never pure black. Emphasis comes from darker ink on key words, not from bold.

Our prototype sets everything in one serif, including long explanations and button-adjacent text. That is the main reason Create and Profile read as heavy.

Our prototype: no hover state on any button, link or card.
