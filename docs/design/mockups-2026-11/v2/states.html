The rules behind the sheet
Hover is a bonusPhones have no pointer. Every hover effect only adds to a control that already looks clickable.
Press always shrinksButtons scale to 96% while held, so a tap feels physical on a phone too.
Focus is always visibleA terracotta ring 3px outside the control, for people who use a keyboard.
Less motion on requestIf the device asks for reduced motion, movement becomes a short fade.
